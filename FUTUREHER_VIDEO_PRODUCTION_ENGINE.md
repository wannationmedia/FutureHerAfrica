## 0. Purpose

This engine is the **repeatable factory** that turns a scored topic into a published YouTube lesson — and the supporting week of Shorts, Reels, and packaging.

### What this engine must never do

- Invent a new process mid-episode  
- Chase polish past the quality gate  
- Skip fact integrity for speed  
- Replace founder voice with synthetic default  
- Redesign CapCut templates during weekly production  

### What this engine always does

- Starts from a real audience problem (Problem Bank / Evergreen Library)  
- Moves through **10 locked stages** in order  
- Uses templates for 80% of look, pace, and packaging  
- Assigns AI to draft / accelerate — human judgement to decide / approve  
- Ends with scheduled publish + a learning signal for next month  

**North star**

> She should finish a FutureHer video knowing exactly what to do next.  
> You should finish production knowing exactly what stage comes next — every week.

## 1. Design Laws

| Law | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pipeline over improvisation** | Stages are sequential. Do not jump to CapCut with an unfacted claim. |
| **Timebox over perfection** | Each stage has a hard stop. Cut scope — never invent process. |
| **Template over redesign** | Duplicate masters. Replace media and text only. |
| **Trust over speed** | Speed comes from system, not from skipping Stage 2. |
| **One master → many outputs** | Long-form creates Shorts/Reels. Never create Shorts from scratch. |
| **One canonical host** | ONE recognisable FutureHerAfrica host · MANY scenes · MANY expressions · SAME person. Authority: `brand/FUTUREHERAFRICA_AI_HOST_BIBLE.md`. Do not invent a new face. |
| **Host where she earns the frame** | Use the host when mentor presence helps. Never force her into diagrams, screen recordings, charts, B-roll, or demos where she adds no value. Human media — not AI wallpaper. |
| **AI drafts · human decides** | AI accelerates research, drafts, SEO options. Founder owns truth, voice, and ethics. |
| **Phone-speaker test** | If audio or captions fail on a phone, the video is not done. |

Publishing is **scheduled**, not live. Batch Sunday → premiere Monday is the default.

## 4. AI vs Human — Master Matrix

| Stage | AI should do | Human must judge |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Research | Angle shortlist, myth scan, SA framing suggestions, autocomplete phrasing | Problem language authenticity; phone-first action; evergreen worth |
| 2 Fact checking | Flag every hard claim; suggest softening patterns | Accept / reject / soften; defendability of every number |
| 3 Script | Cold-open options, clarity rewrite, Shorts hook extraction | Final spoken lines; one action; tone; SA English naturalness |
| 4 Voice-over | Optional breathability shorten; CapCut denoise/enhance | Performance energy; mentor presence; never default to TTS |
| 5 Visual planning | Shot-list draft; stock keywords; non-host AI prompts; host prompt fills from bible template | Taste; host squint test; stereotype rejection; license safety; max 3 non-host AI stills; host only where editorial |
| 6 CapCut | Auto captions; enhance; template assembly | Caption SA names; pace; brand feel; kill-switch if redesigning |
| 7 Thumbnail | 8 text options (max 6 words) | Stamp-size readability; canonical host face (not a new woman); no clickbait; series pill correct |
| 8 SEO | Title variants; chapter labels; tag shortlist | Truthful click promise; her language over cleverness |
| 9 Publishing | — (paste packs; Studio schedule) | QC gate pass; schedule integrity; disclosures |
| 10 Analytics | Summarise notes → Keep / Fix / Stop | Exactly one operational change; ignore vanity traps |

**Hard rule:** AI never publishes. AI never owns factual claims. AI never replaces founder voice as the channel default.

### Stage 1 — Research

**Objective:** Collect just enough truth, South African context, and audience language to teach one idea, one skill, and one action.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Timebox** | 20 minutes (hard stop) |
| **SOP** | `operations/SOPs/01_RESEARCH.md` |
| **Template** | `templates/EPISODE_BRIEF.md` |
| **Inputs** | Problem Bank #1 · Topic Card / Evergreen ID · last comments |
| **Output** | ≤ 5 research bullets + problem sentence + phone-first action + CapCut template A–E |

#### Checklist

- [ ] Open Problem Bank (or Evergreen Library) → select this week’s problem  
- [ ] Write the **problem sentence** in her exact words  
- [ ] Assign pillar + CapCut template (A–E) + series accent  
- [ ] AI: shortlist 5 teachable angles; reject hustle/fear framing  
- [ ] Choose **one** angle only  
- [ ] Gather ≤ 5 supporting points (example · myth · SA context · tool · constraint)  
- [ ] Note one phone-first action she can finish in < 30 minutes  
- [ ] Psychology beat in **one sentence** (support only — not clinical)  
- [ ] List source links for any statistic / official claim (max 3)  
- [ ] Fill Episode Brief meta + research section  
- [ ] Stop at 20 minutes  

#### AI tasks

| Tool | Task |
|---|---|
| ChatGPT | Angle shortlist, myth detection, SA framing |
| YouTube search | Autocomplete / “people also ask” phrasing only |

#### Human judgement

1. Is this *her* language — or creator jargon?  
2. Is the action doable on a phone this week?  
3. Would you still teach this with zero trending data?

#### Exit criteria

Brief research section complete · ≤ 5 bullets · sources noted · ready for Stage 2.

### Stage 2 — Fact checking

**Objective:** Prevent trust damage. Verify claims **before** they become voice-over. Prefer understatement over impressive falsehoods.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Timebox** | 5–10 minutes |
| **SOP** | `operations/SOPs/03_FACT_CHECKING.md` |
| **Template** | Episode Brief source list (then script `FACT-OK` after Stage 3) |
| **Inputs** | Research bullets + any planned stats / tool / money / career claims |
| **Output** | Claims verified, softened, or removed · research status `CLAIMS-CLEARED` |

> **Order note:** Fact-check research *before* scripting (this engine). After the script draft in Stage 3, run a 2-minute claim sweep and stamp `FACT-OK` on the script before recording.

#### Checklist

- [ ] Highlight every number, “studies show”, “most women”, tool capability, money/return claim  
- [ ] For each: verify on primary source **or** soften to lived observation  
- [ ] Money/fintech: no guaranteed returns; no illegal product advice  
- [ ] AI: state limits; no fearmongering replacement narratives  
- [ ] Career: no fake salary guarantees  
- [ ] Quotes: accurate attribution or remove  
- [ ] SA context: ZAR, platforms, laws — no blind US defaults  
- [ ] If unverifiable in 2 minutes → rewrite as opinion/experience  
- [ ] Mark brief `CLAIMS-CLEARED`  

#### Softening patterns

| Risky | Safer |
|---|---|
| “Studies show 90%…” | “Many women tell me…” / “In practice I see…” |
| “This will double your income” | “This can clarify your next money decision” |
| “ChatGPT always…” | “In many cases ChatGPT can…” |

#### AI tasks

| Tool | Task |
|---|---|
| ChatGPT | “List every factual claim that needs a source” |
| Web / official docs | Spot-check only |

#### Human judgement

1. Would you defend this claim with a sceptic?  
2. Does softening increase trust more than a flashy number?

#### Exit criteria

All hard claims sourced or softened · no clinical prescriptions · no get-rich implications · `CLAIMS-CLEARED`.

### Stage 5 — Visual planning

**Objective:** Decide the visual plan *before* editing — so CapCut is assembly, not invention. Cap at a small visual set. Maintain **one canonical host** continuity when a host visual is needed.

| | |
|---|---|

| **Output** | Shot / asset list · host frames only where editorial · ≤ 3 non-host AI stills if needed · ≤ 5 stock inserts · assets in episode folder |

#### Canonical host capability (per episode — generate only what the plan needs)

The system must be *capable* of producing these host visuals for every episode. Generate only the ones that earn a frame:

| Host visual | Typical use |
|---|---|
| Talking-head | Hook / teaching presence cutaway |
| Instructional | Calm “here’s how” mentor insert |
| Reaction | Soft knowing look on a reframe beat |
| Explanatory | Thoughtful focus while a framework lands |
| Thumbnail pose | Stage 7 face lock (same woman) |
| Shorts frame | Vertical hook / reaction still or punch-in source |
| Social graphic | Mentor face moment on quote/teach cards when needed |
| Presentation visual | Deck opener / Photo slide — not on charts |

**Do not force the host into:** technical diagrams · screen recordings · B-roll · demonstrations · charts · any scene where her presence adds no value.

**Continuity law:** ONE CANONICAL HOST · MANY SCENES · MANY EXPRESSIONS · SAME RECOGNIZABLE PERSON.  
Reference: `launch/EP001/FH_EP001_Thumbnail.png` (thumbnail wins if conflict).

#### Checklist

- [ ] Duplicate `VISUAL_PLAN.md` into episode folder  
- [ ] Map each script section → visual type (A-roll / screen / B-roll / diagram / host / AI still)  
- [ ] Mark host slots only where mentor presence helps understanding or packaging  
- [ ] Prefer own B-roll → CapCut stock → AI still (max 3 non-host)  
- [ ] List max **5** moments that need non-face visuals  
- [ ] Reject stereotype, poverty porn, fake luxury, shocked-face stock  
- [ ] If generating host: use `FH_HOST_GENERATION_PROMPT.txt` + EP001 reference; run squint test  
- [ ] Generate non-host AI stills only if a concept cannot be taught on A-roll  
- [ ] Log AI prompts beside files for reuse (seed + reference filename for host)  
- [ ] Confirm license-safe for YouTube monetization  
- [ ] Stop at 15 minutes — teach with A-roll if assets fail  

#### AI tasks

| Tool | Task |
|---|---|
| ChatGPT | Shot-list draft from script; stock keyword list; image prompts |
| Host gen (any platform) | Fill `FH_HOST_GENERATION_PROMPT.txt` variables; attach EP001 reference |
| CapCut / Canva AI | Generative stills sparingly (non-host); host only with reference lock |
| CapCut library | Primary stock search |

#### Human judgement

1. Does this look like *her* world?  
2. Would removing it hurt understanding? If no → skip.  
3. If a host face appears: is it the **same** EP001 woman — or a similar stranger? Fail → regenerate.  
4. Would any AI face mislead as “real footage”? Label as digital host / still, or don’t use.

#### Exit criteria

Visual plan filled · host slots justified or skipped · assets gathered or consciously skipped · ready for CapCut replace-only edit.

2. Any disclosure missing (affiliate / sponsor)?

#### Exit criteria

Week queued · QC = PASS · tracker = `SCHEDULED`.

---

### Stage 10 — Analytics

**Objective:** Extract **one** operational decision from data — what to repeat, fix, or stop — without making analytics a second job.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Timebox** | 30 minutes |
| **Cadence** | **Monthly** (not weekly) |
| **SOP** | `operations/SOPs/12_ANALYTICS_REVIEW.md` |
| **Template** | `08_Analytics/monthly-log` (Drive sheet) |
| **Inputs** | YouTube Studio 28 days · Episode Tracker · comments |
| **Output** | Keep / Fix / Stop · one change for next month · Problem Bank updates |

#### Checklist

- [ ] Open YouTube Analytics → Last 28 days  
- [ ] Note: impressions CTR, average view duration %, top 2 traffic sources  
- [ ] Identify top 3 and bottom 3 by retained audience  
- [ ] Top 3: problem language + template A–E + hook pattern  
- [ ] Bottom 3: topic wrong, hook weak, or packaging off?  
- [ ] Shorts → long-form funnel check  
- [ ] Skim comments → ≤ 10 Problem Bank additions  
- [ ] Decide **one** change for next month only  
- [ ] Log: date, keep, fix, stop  
- [ ] Refresh CTA links if broken  
- [ ] Stop at 30 minutes  

#### Decision rules

| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| High CTR, low retention | Fix cold open / pacing — not thumbnails alone |
| Low CTR, good retention | Fix title/thumbnail packaging |
| Shorts views, no subs | Strengthen end CTA + series continuity |
| Comments ask same Q | Make that next episode |

#### AI tasks

| Tool | Task |
|---|---|
| ChatGPT | Paste notes → Keep / Fix / Stop summary |
| YouTube Studio | Source of truth |

#### Human judgement

1. One concrete tweak — or a new process addiction?  
2. Chasing vanity spikes over trust topics?

#### Exit criteria

Exactly one change committed · Problem Bank updated · logged · resume weekly production.

## 9. Quality System

### 9.1 Stage exit gates

No stage starts until the previous exit criteria are met. Exception: Stage 5 may be marked `SKIP — A-roll sufficient`.

### 9.2 Pre-publish gate (mandatory before Stage 9 go-live)

Run `operations/QC/PRE_PUBLISH_QUALITY_GATE.md`:

Accuracy · Clarity · South African relevance · Accessibility · Professional language · Ethical recommendations · Brand consistency · Copyright · Platform policy.

**Fail → fix that item only.** Do not reopen creative direction.

### 9.3 Premium quality definition (FutureHer)

Premium does **not** mean more effects. It means:

- Correct facts (or honest softening)  
- Calm mentor pacing  
- One idea · one skill · one action  
- Captions + audio that work on a phone  
- Brand-consistent packaging  
- Respect for her intelligence and her constraints

## 10. Kill-Switches & Failure Modes

| Failure | Kill-switch |
|---|---|
| Research > 20 min | Cap at 5 bullets; teach from experience |
| Fact rabbit hole | Soften claim; move on |
| Script > 30 min | Ship readable draft; no rewrite loops |
| VO energy low | Cold open retake only — don’t remake whole lesson |
| Visual planning stall | Skip to A-roll-only edit |
| CapCut long-form > 45 min | You are designing — revert to template |
| Thumbnail workshopping | Pick one AI text option; export |
| SEO perfectionism | Use title templates; ship |
| Analytics paralysis | Force one change; close laptop |

| Systemic failure | Fix |
|---|---|
| Missed week | Shorts-only from last markers; never triple long-form catch-up |
| Tool FOMO | Re-read Operating System §2.3 |
| Quality anxiety | Consistency is the premium signal |
