[0:25 EVERYDAY SA STORY — ~60s]

Ake ucabange lokhu.
Usesema ofisini eGoli — noma usebenza egumbini lakho ePolokwane —
bese othile efaka icebo le-ChatGPT eqenjini le-WhatsApp njengokuthi akulutho.

Ingxenye yeqembu ithula.
Othile uhlekisa.
Omunye uthi "sonke sizothathelwa indawo ngokhisimusi."

Uhleka nawo.
Kodwa ngalobo busuku uhlole izikhala zomsebenzi ongazidingi —
ukuze nje uzizwe uphephile.

Lokhu kwenzeka kothisha, ezikhulu zokuphatha, abadayisi, abasebenzi bamabhange, abazimele —
abesifazane abaqhakambile —
kodwa besazizwa sengathi umhlabathi uyanyakaza.

Iqiniso elilula:
usuku lwakho lomsebenzi lukhiqizwa imisebenzi emincane.

[1:25 THE PROBLEM — ~80s] [S2]

ON-SCREEN: Fear ≠ forecast

Inkinga akusiyo ukuthi i-AI ikhona.
Inkinga ukuthi siphatha i-AI njengombiko wesimo sezulu ngemisebenzi yethu —
bese siqina esikhundleni sokwenza.

Ukwesaba ngaphandle kohlelo kuba ukugwema.
Ukugwema kuba igebe lamakhono.
Igebe lamakhono liba ingozi yangempela.

Hlukanisa izinto ezintathu:

Eyokuqala — Izihloko zezindaba zidayisa ukwesaba.
Eyesibili — Umsebenzi wakho yiqoqo lemisebenzi.
Eyesithathu — Eminye ingasizwa. Eminye ayikwazi.

I-AI inobuchwepheshe ekubhaleni, ekufingqeni, ekuhleleni, nasekucwaningeni.
I-AI imbi ekuthwaleni imiphumela, ekufundeni indawo, ekugcineni ithemba, nasekuthwaleni isibopho.

Uma inani lakho lingukubhala nje —
lelo zinga lingaphansi kwengcindezi.

Uma inani lakho lihlanganisa ukwahlulela, ubudlelwano, umongo, nokuziphendulela —
awusapheli.
Uyamenywa ukuthi uthuthukise.

ON-SCREEN: Tasks under pressure ≠ you under pressure

[2:45 THE SKILL — ~100s] [S3]

Framework name: THE TASK LAYER

Nali ikhono: i-Task Layer.

Isinyathelo 1 — Dweba imephu.
Bhala imisebenzi emihlanu oyenzile kuleli sonto.
Hhayi isikhundla. Imisebenzi.
"Ngisaphendule ama-imeyili."
"Ngakha ispreadsheet."
"Ngahola umhlangano."
"Ngalungisa isikhalazo."
"Ngalungiselela umbiko."

Isinyathelo 2 — Faka ilebula.
ASSIST — i-AI ingasheshisa
OWN — kudinga ukwahlulela kwami
HYBRID — hlaka nge-AI, bese ngiyazinquma

Abesifazane abaningi bathola ukuthula lapha:
ingxenye enkulu yokwesaba inamathele ku-ASSIST —
kanti inani labo lihlezi ku-OWN naku-HYBRID.

Isinyathelo 3 — Sebenzisa eyodwa.
Khetha umsebenzi owodwa we-ASSIST noma we-HYBRID.
Cela i-AI ihlake inguqulo yokuqala —
bese wena uhlela ngeqiniso, ngezwi, nangomongo.

Lokho yi-Task Layer.
Uyeka ukuphikisana nekusasa okungacacile —
uqhuba umsebenzi onawo ngempela.

DEMO NOTES:
Show phone screen: 5 tasks → labels → one email draft → human edit.

ON-SCREEN: Map · Label · Run one
