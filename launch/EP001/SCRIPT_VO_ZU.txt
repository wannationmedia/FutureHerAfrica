Uma ubusuke uskidla izihloko ze-AI bese uzwa isisu sakho siyehla —
awudlalisi.
Ufunda indawo.

Ukwesaba akusiyo into ethi "ngisalele ngemuva."
Ukwesaba kuthi: "Ingabe kusazoba nendawo yami?"

Namuhla ngizokunikeza umbono owodwa, ikhono elilodwa, kanye nesenzo esisodwa ongasiqeda ngaphambi kwesidlo sasemini.
Akusona isikhuthazo. Ukulungela.

[0:30 EVERYDAY SA STORY — ~90s]

Ake ucabange lokhu.
Usesema ofisini avulekile eGoli — noma usebenza egumbini lakho ePolokwane — bese othile efaka icebo le-ChatGPT eqenjini le-WhatsApp njengokuthi akulutho.

Ingxenye yeqembu ithula.
Othile uhlekisa.
Omunye uthi "sonke sizothathelwa indawo ngokhisimusi."

Uhleka nawo.
Kodwa ngalobo busuku uhlole izikhala zomsebenzi ongazidingi —
ukuze nje uzizwe uphephile.

Lokhu ngikubonile kothisha, ezikhulu zokuphatha, abadayisi, abasebenzi bamabhange, abazimele —
abesifazane abaqhakambile emisebenzini yabo —
kodwa besazizwa sengathi umhlabathi uyanyakaza ngaphansi kwabo.

Nali iqiniso okungathiwa ngobukhulu:
abantu abethuka kakhulu ngokuvamile basabela ezinhlokweni zezindaba,
hhayi emsebenzini wabo wangempela wosuku.

Usuku lwakho lomsebenzi lukhiqizwa imisebenzi emincane.
Akusiyo into eyodwa yokuthi ungubani i-AI engayisusa ngobusuku obubodwa.

[2:00 THE PROBLEM — ~120s] [S2]

ON-SCREEN: Fear ≠ forecast

Inkinga akusiyo ukuthi i-AI ikhona.
Inkinga ukuthi siphatha i-AI njengombiko wesimo sezulu ngemisebenzi yethu —
bese siqina esikhundleni sokwenza.

Ukwesaba ngaphandle kohlelo kuba ukugwema.
Ukugwema kuba igebe lamakhono.
Igebe lamakhono liba ingozi yangempela.

Ngakho ake sihlukanise izinto ezintathu ezivame ukuxutshwa:

Eyokuqala — Izihloko zezindaba zidayisa ukwesaba.
Eyesibili — Umsebenzi wakho yiqoqo lemisebenzi.
Eyesithathu — Eminye yaleyo misebenzi ingasizwa. Eminye ayikwazi.

I-AI inobuchwepheshe ekubhaleni okusalungiswa, ekufingqeni, ekuhleleni, ekucwaningeni, nasekubhaleni kabusha.
I-AI imbi kakhulu ekuthwaleni imiphumela, ekufundeni indawo, ekugcineni ithemba, ekwenzeni izinqumo ngaphansi kwengcindezi, nasekuthwaleni isibopho uma into yephuka.

Uma inani lakho lingukuthi "ngingabhala lokho othile angitshele ukuthi ngikulobe" —
yebo, lelo zinga lingaphansi kwengcindezi.

Uma inani lakho lihlanganisa ukwahlulela, ubudlelwano, umongo, kanye nokuziphendulela —
awusapheli.
Uyamenywa ukuthi uthuthukise ingxenye yomsebenzi eyiseyingakwazi ukuthwalwa yimishini.

[4:00 THE SKILL — ~120s] [S3]

Framework name: THE TASK LAYER

Nali ikhono.

Isinyathelo 1 — Dweba imephu.
Bhala phansi imisebenzi emihlanu oyenzile emsebenzini kuleli sonto.
Hhayi isikhundla sakho. Imisebenzi.
"Ngisaphendule ama-imeyili amakhasimende."
"Ngakha ispreadsheet."
"Ngahola umhlangano."
"Ngalungisa isikhalazo."
"Ngalungiselela umbiko."

Isinyathelo 2 — Faka ilebula.
Eceleni komsebenzi ngamunye, bhala igama elilodwa:
ASSIST — i-AI ingasheshisa lokhu
OWN — lokhu kudinga ukwahlulela kwami
noma HYBRID — hlaka nge-AI, bese ngiyazinquma mina.

Abesifazane abaningi bathola okuthile okuzolisa lapha:
ingxenye enkulu yokwesaba kwabo inamathele emisebenzini ye-ASSIST —
kanti inani labo langempela lihlezi ku-OWN naku-HYBRID.

Isinyathelo 3 — Sebenzisa eyodwa.
Khetha umsebenzi owodwa we-ASSIST noma we-HYBRID.
Vula ucingo lwakho noma ilaptop.
Cela i-AI ukuba ihlake inguqulo yokuqala —
bese wena uhlela ngeqiniso, ngezwi, nangomongo.

Lokho yi-Task Layer.
Uyeka ukuphikisana nekusasa okungacacile —
bese uqala ukuzijwayeza umsebenzi onawo ngempela.
