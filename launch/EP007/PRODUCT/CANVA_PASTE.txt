── PAGE 4: VERIFY teach ──
Teach: Source: can this claim live outside the chat on a real page or your own file? Number: confirm every percentage, date, price, and statistic — or cut it. Name: check people, organisations, laws, places, and titles before send. Correct or cut — then share.




── PAGE 5: Source gate ──
Teach: Ask: where does this live outside the chat?
Prompt 1: Source I used for today's key claim:
☐ I can point to a real page, policy, or my own file for this claim
☐ I did not accept a citation I have not opened
☐ If the source is missing, I cut the claim or marked it as unverified
☐ I separated AI wording from evidence I actually hold


── PAGE 6: Number + Name gates ──
Teach: Statistics lie fluently. Names travel forever.
Prompt 1: Number I verified:
Prompt 2: Name I double-checked:
☐ Every percentage is confirmed or removed
☐ Every date, price, and count is confirmed or removed
☐ People names are spelled correctly
☐ Organisation, school, clinic, or company names are real
☐ Law / policy titles are checked before I teach or send them

── ACTION SHEETS ──
1. Pause one AI paste before it travels (1 min) — Deck, email, or chat — pause for VERIFY.
2. Run the Source gate on one claim (5 min) — Find it outside the chat or cut it.
3. Circle every number and confirm or cut (7 min) — No confirmation → no number.
4. Check every name and title (5 min) — Spelling and reality.
5. Fill one row on the claim scorecard (5 min) — Action = keep, fix, or cut.
6. Text a colleague Source · Number · Name (2 min) — Teach the seatbelt — culture beats tools.
