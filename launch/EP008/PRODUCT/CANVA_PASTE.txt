── PAGE 4: Payday Map teach ──
Teach: Fixed: must-pay costs that keep life standing — include transport, data, airtime basics, and a groceries baseline. Flexible: choices that change week to week — give them a ceiling. Forward: money that leaves today's pressure — save, stokvel, buffer, or extra debt payment. Unassigned money gets spent by the loudest notification.

── ACTION SHEETS ──
1. Write this month's income on one line (3 min) — One number. No story attached yet.
2. Build your Fixed list including transport and data (15 min) — Must-pay costs that keep life standing.
3. Set a Flexible ceiling (5 min) — Choices need a lid.
4. Choose a Forward amount — even if small (5 min) — Save, stokvel, buffer, or debt extra.
5. Assign every rand a job within 48 hours of payday (20 min) — Map while numbers are clear.
6. Complete Days 1–3 of the 14-day planner (25 min) — Momentum over perfection.
7. Use the week-three rescue page once if needed (10 min) — Categories before shame.
