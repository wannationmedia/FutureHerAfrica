── PAGE 4: Trust Stack overview ──
Teach: The Trust Stack has three gates. Need: one job for AI this month. Safety: three non-negotiable rules you can explain in plain English. Stick: one primary assistant for thirty days while you mute the rest. Free can be fine. Paid can be fine. Fluency needs repetition.




── PAGE 5: Need checklist ──
Teach: Shrink the job until it is honest. One job beats ten shiny features.
Prompt 1: My one Need sentence:
☐ I named one work job for AI this month — not ten
☐ The job shows up in my real week (emails, notes, outlines, sorting)
☐ I can finish a first attempt on phone or laptop I already have
☐ I am not choosing a tool only because a WhatsApp group hyped it


── PAGE 6: Safety checklist ──
Teach: If you cannot explain where your data goes in one plain sentence — pause.
Prompt 1: My three safety rules:
☐ Never paste passwords or OTPs into AI
☐ Never paste private client, learner, or payroll files without permission
☐ Always edit facts before I send or submit
☐ I paused on any tool asking for access I do not understand
☐ I know whether this tool is allowed at my workplace

── ACTION SHEETS ──
1. Write your one Need sentence (5 min) — One job only — emails, notes, outlines, or sorting.
2. Write three safety rules in Notes (5 min) — Passwords, private files, edit-before-send.
3. Score every AI app currently on your phone (10 min) — Use the tool scorecard. Be honest.
4. Choose one primary tool for 30 days (3 min) — ChatGPT, Gemini, or Copilot — circle one.
5. Mute or delete unused AI apps (5 min) — Remove the circus so Stick can happen.
6. Run one real task on the primary tool today (15 min) — Save the result. That is fluency starting.
