── PAGE 4: Micro-Save system ──
Teach: Floor: the smallest repeatable amount you will not lie about. Automate: payday auto-move or same-day manual ritual — stokvel counts if it is your system. Protect: one rule that stops impulse and pressure from raiding it for 30 days. Shrink until honesty fits. Then guard the streak.




── PAGE 5: Floor · Automate · Protect setup ──
Teach: Write the three lines before pressure arrives.
Prompt 1: My floor (R) — so small it almost feels too easy:
Prompt 2: Move method (auto-move / same-hour ritual / stokvel):
Prompt 3: Protect rule (example: I wait 72 hours before any raid):
Prompt 4: Where the floor lives:
☐ My floor is honest for a hard month, not a fantasy month
☐ I scheduled the move for next payday
☐ I named stokvel, solo, or both for this season


── PAGE 6: 30-day streak tracker ──
Teach: Tick the move. Protect the streak. No performance theatre.




── PAGE 7: Pressure & raid scripts ──
Teach: Protect the streak with warm, short language.


[To yourself] This floor is small on purpose. Missing it for a treat I will forget is how the old story wins. I wait 72 hours.
[Soft decline on spending pressure] I'm keeping a tiny save streak this month. I can't pull from it today — let's look at another option tomorrow.
[Stokvel honour line] My contribution is Forward money. I treat it as already spent toward future-me.

── ACTION SHEETS ──
1. Choose a floor so small it almost feels too easy (5 min) — Honesty over impressiveness.
2. Set the payday move method (8 min) — Auto-move, same-hour ritual, or stokvel path.
3. Write one Protect rule (5 min) — Example: wait 72 hours before any raid.
4. Move the first floor amount (5 min) — Before Flexible expands.
5. Log the move on the streak tracker (2 min) — Date · amount · where · protected.
6. Practise one pressure script aloud (3 min) — Say it once before you need it.
