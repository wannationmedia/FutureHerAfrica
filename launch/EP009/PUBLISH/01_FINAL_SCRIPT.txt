THE PROBLEM (2:07–4:07) [S2]
═══════════════════════════════════════
VISUAL: Calm B-roll · kinetic type · lower-thirds
ON-SCREEN: Ideal budgets fail

The problem isn't that you're undisciplined.
The problem is ideal savings targets that collapse on contact with real life.

When the target is fantasy, missing it becomes a personality story:
"I always fail."

So change the design:

Floor — the smallest amount you can repeat without lying to yourself.
Automate — move it on payday or the same day you get paid, before Flexible expands.
Protect — rules that stop impulse and pressure from raiding it for thirty days.

Celebration is allowed.
A kept R50 promise beats an abandoned R2000 speech.

═══════════════════════════════════════
THE SKILL (4:07–6:07) [S3]
═══════════════════════════════════════
VISUAL: Title card THE MICRO-SAVE · Pathway 01/02/03 · screen demo
ON-SCREEN: Floor · Automate · Protect
DEMO: Choose floor R50 → move to savings pocket → write Protect rule in Notes

Here's the skill.
Framework name: THE MICRO-SAVE

Step 1 — Floor.
Choose an amount so small it almost feels too easy — then keep it.
Examples: R20 airtime-equivalent redirected; R50 on payday; R100 if your map truly allows.
Write: "My floor this month is R__."

Step 2 — Automate.
If your bank can auto-move to a savings pocket — set it.
If not, manual is fine: payday ritual, same hour, same place in Notes.
Stokvel contributions count as a Forward system — map them with respect.

Step 3 — Protect.
Write one Protect rule:
"I don't touch Micro-Save for takeaways."
"I wait 72 hours before any raid."
"If I must use it for a true emergency, I restart the streak without insulting myself."

That is the Micro-Save.
Floor. Automate. Protect.
You stop waiting to feel rich —
and you start keeping a promise your nervous system can believe.
