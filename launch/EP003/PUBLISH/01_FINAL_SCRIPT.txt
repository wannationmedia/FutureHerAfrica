Picture this.
You're in a taxi between Polokwane and work —
or sitting through load-shedding with one bar of signal —
and someone online says "just open your laptop and build a workflow."

You don't have a laptop.
Or you do, but it's at home, or the battery is dead, or data is expensive.

So you tell yourself you'll start later.
Later becomes never.
Meanwhile the same women with fancy setups keep posting screenshots.

I've seen rural learners, first-job women, and busy mothers
finish real AI tasks on a phone between chores —
not because they had more data —
because they had a smaller system.

Here's the truth:
device envy is expensive.
A finished phone task is progress.

THE PROBLEM (2:07–4:07) [S2]
═══════════════════════════════════════
VISUAL: Calm B-roll · kinetic type · lower-thirds
ON-SCREEN: No laptop ≠ no AI

The problem isn't that phones are weak.
The problem is we treat AI like a desktop hobby.

We wait for the perfect setup:
big screen, unlimited Wi‑Fi, quiet office, free afternoon.

That waiting becomes avoidance.
Avoidance becomes skill gap.
Skill gap becomes the real disadvantage.

So let's separate three things:

One — A laptop is nice. It is not required for a first AI win.
Two — Data is a budget line. Work inside windows you can afford.
Three — Capture offline, prompt when signal allows, save immediately.

Low-data mode is not lesser.
It is intelligent.

If your AI habit only works on perfect Wi‑Fi,
you don't have a habit —
you have a hope.

THE SKILL (4:07–6:07) [S3]
═══════════════════════════════════════
VISUAL: Title card THE PHONE FLOW · Pathway 01/02/03 · screen demo
ON-SCREEN: Capture · Prompt · Save
DEMO: Voice note → mobile AI prompt → save edited result in Notes

Here's the skill.
Framework name: THE PHONE FLOW

Step 1 — Capture.
Before you open AI, capture the task.
Voice note: "Draft a polite WhatsApp to reschedule Tuesday's meeting to Thursday 10:00."
Or type three messy lines in Notes while offline.
Capture is cheap. Perfect wording can wait.

Step 2 — Prompt.
When you have Wi‑Fi or a data window, open your one AI tool.
Paste or speak a clear ask:
role, task, context, and keep it short.
One prompt. One job.
Don't browse ten features on mobile data.

Step 3 — Save.
Copy the useful parts into Notes before you close the app.
Edit two lines in your voice.
Screenshot only if you must — Notes is cleaner and searchable.

That is the Phone Flow.
Capture. Prompt. Save.
You stop waiting for a laptop fantasy —
and you finish work on the phone you already own.
