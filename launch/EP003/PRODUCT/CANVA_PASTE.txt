── PAGE 4: Phone Flow system ──
Teach: The Phone Flow is three moves that fit a smartphone. Capture: voice note or three messy lines before you open AI. Prompt: one clear ask in a Wi‑Fi or short data window. Save: copy the useful result into Notes and edit two lines in your voice before you switch apps.




── PAGE 5: Low-data playbook ──
Teach: Treat data like airtime — planned, not endless scroll.
Prompt 1: My usual data window today (time + place):
Prompt 2: Task I will finish phone-only:
☐ I captured the task offline or on Notes before opening AI
☐ I chose one Wi‑Fi or short data window for prompting
☐ I avoided browsing extra AI features during that window
☐ I saved the useful output before the app switched or signal dropped


── PAGE 6: Voice-to-prompt cards ──
Teach: Talk first. Type less. A messy voice note is a valid first draft of your prompt.


[Capture script] I need help with [task]. Audience is [who]. Must include [facts]. Tone should sound like me at work — clear, warm, no buzzwords.
[Prompt wrapper] Turn my notes into [deliverable] under 120 words. Keep my facts. Do not invent names or numbers. End with one clear ask.
[Save checklist whisper] Before I close: copy to Notes · edit two lines · delete anything untrue · done.

── PAGE 7: Weekly phone tracker ──
Teach: Five loops. Phone only. That is a week of readiness.




── ACTION SHEETS ──
1. Capture one task as a voice note or three lines (3 min) — Do this before you open any AI app.
2. Choose today's data window (2 min) — Lunch Wi‑Fi, evening home, or one short mobile burst.
3. Prompt once on your phone (8 min) — One ask. No feature tourism.
4. Save and edit two lines in Notes (7 min) — Make it sound like you before you close.
5. Log the loop on the weekly tracker (3 min) — Task · method · where you saved it.
6. Repeat one more loop before the week ends (15 min) — Fluency is repetition on a phone you already own.
