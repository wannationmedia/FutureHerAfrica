── PAGE 4: Work Map teach ──
Teach: Automate: repetitive low-risk tasks where mistakes are easy to spot and fix. Assist: AI drafts, you verify, you own the send. Avoid: passwords, confidential data, invented facts, final judgments, and unsupervised commitments. Ambiguity feels like danger — a map restores movement.




── PAGE 5: Duty mapping worksheet ──
Teach: List eight real duties from your job. Label each lane. Note what you must check before share.




── PAGE 6: Manager conversation scripts ──
Teach: Use these when policy is vague. Warm. Clear. Short. No drama.


[Clarify the lane] I want to use AI in a way that protects our work. For [duty], I plan to use it as Assist — AI drafts, I verify. Are there duties you want marked Avoid?
[Privacy boundary] I will not paste passwords, OTPs, payroll, or private client/learner data into AI tools. If a task needs those details, I will keep it human-only.
[Verification habit] Before I share AI-assisted work, I will verify sources, numbers, and names. If I cannot verify, I will cut the claim.

── ACTION SHEETS ──
1. List eight real duties from your job (10 min) — Concrete duties — not vague goals.
2. Label each Automate, Assist, or Avoid (8 min) — When unsure, choose Avoid until clarified.
3. Write one manager clarification message (7 min) — Use a script from this guide — edit in your voice.
4. Run one Assist task with AI (12 min) — Draft, verify, then share only after checks.
5. Remove one sensitive detail before prompting (3 min) — Names, IDs, or private numbers out.
