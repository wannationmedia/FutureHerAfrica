── PAGE 4: Edit Loop teach ──
Teach: Draft: let AI start the structure — ask for no buzzwords from the beginning when you can. Strip: delete empty openers and corporate camouflage you would never say aloud. Stamp: add one true detail only you would know, then read aloud before you send.




── PAGE 5: Strip library ──
Teach: Kill the camouflage. These phrases rarely sound like a real person in Braamfontein, Polokwane, or Durban.




── PAGE 6: Stamp templates ──
Teach: Stamp truth. Invoice numbers, real meeting details, real reasons — that is trust.


[Email stamp] Add: the real meeting day, the exact ask, and one reason that is true. Remove any sentence you would not say to a colleague face to face.
[Report stamp] Replace vague claims with one verified number from your own notes. If you cannot verify it, cut it.
[WhatsApp / short message stamp] One warm greeting · one clear fact · one ask · no corporate filler.

── PAGE 7: Before/after worksheet ──
Teach: Practise on a real draft — school letter, client email, or manager update.
Prompt 1: Read-aloud note — where did my mouth trip?



── ACTION SHEETS ──
1. Open your last AI draft (2 min) — Email, report, or message — anything you almost sent.
2. Highlight three lines that are not you (5 min) — If you would not say it aloud, mark it.
3. Strip five banned phrases into your strip list (8 min) — Write natural replacements beside each.
4. Stamp one true detail only you know (5 min) — Meeting fact, invoice detail, or real reason.
5. Read the final paragraph aloud once (3 min) — If your mouth trips, edit again.
6. Save the before/after in your edit log (3 min) — Reuse the pattern tomorrow.
