── PAGE 4: Kind No teach ──
Teach: Pause: non-emergencies get 24 hours — check your plan before your guilt spends. Values: need, want, or pressure? Does it fit your Payday Map? Will you respect yourself after? Script: warm, clear, short Kind No — practised out loud before you send. Belonging is not a receipt.




── PAGE 5: Values pages ──
Teach: Label the spend before the story writes itself.
Prompt 1: Request or purchase in front of me:
Prompt 2: Is this a need, a want, or pressure?
Prompt 3: Does it fit my Payday Map this month?
Prompt 4: Will I respect myself after a yes?
Prompt 5: Will I respect myself after a Kind No?

── PAGE 6: Script bank ──
Teach: Practise out loud once. Then send. Warm. Clear. Short.


[Pause reply] I need to check my plan — I'll come back tomorrow.
[Kind No — outing] Thank you for including me. I can't spend on this one, and I still care about you. Let's find a low-cost catch-up soon.
[Kind No — family ask] I hear you. I'm not able to help with money on this one. I can help think through options if that is useful.
[Values yes] I've checked my plan — yes, this fits what matters to me this month. I'm in.

── ACTION SHEETS ──
1. Start a 24-hour pause on one non-emergency ask (3 min) — Timestamp tomorrow's decision time in Notes.
2. Label it need, want, or pressure (5 min) — Use the values page — no rush.
3. Check the ask against your Payday Map (5 min) — If you have no map yet, open Money Clarity.
4. Practise one Kind No script out loud (3 min) — Mouth first, then send.
5. Send the calm decision before guilt writes it (5 min) — Yes on values or Kind No — both can be respectful.
6. Log the decision in the journal table (3 min) — Pattern over self-attack.
