# Email Sequence Template — Digital Products

**Cadence:** 5 emails over 10 days after purchase or soft-launch opt-in.  
**Voice:** FutureHer mentor — calm, practical, South African English.  
**Engine:** Generated per product in `launch/EPxxx/PRODUCT/EMAIL_SEQUENCE.txt`

---

## Sequence map

| # | Day | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | Deliver + first open action (≤15 min) |
| 2 | 2 | Framework teach + one practice prompt |
| 3 | 4 | Obstacle / psychology reframe |
| 4 | 7 | Action sheet push + win story prompt |
| 5 | 10 | Soft upsell / Forward Collective invite |

---

## Hard rules

1. Subject ≤ 7 words. No spammy ALL CAPS.  
2. One CTA per email.  
3. No fake urgency. Readiness compounds.  
4. Reuse episode framework — do not invent new IP.  
5. Every email ends with: `Ready for what's next. — FutureHer`
