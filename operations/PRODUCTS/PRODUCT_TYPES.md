# Product Types — Digital Product Engine

**Rule:** Every episode ships one digital product. Type is chosen for the skill shape — not for variety theatre.  
**Engine:** `scripts/generate-products.mjs` → `launch/EPxxx/PRODUCT/`

---

## Allowed types

| Type | Best when the skill is… | Typical length | Price band (ZAR) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Workbook** | Multi-step practice with write-in depth | 12–16 pages | R149–R197 |
| **Checklist** | Binary / pass-fail decisions | 8–10 pages | R79–R97 |
| **Planner** | Dated or sequenced days/weeks | 12–18 pages | R149–R247 |
| **Journal** | Reflection + scripts over time | 10–14 pages | R129–R179 |
| **Template** | Fill-and-reuse blocks (emails, prompts) | 8–12 pages | R97–R149 |
| **Guide** | Teach + decide + act (policy clarity) | 10–14 pages | R149–R197 |
| **Cheat Sheet** | One-glance reference kept forever | 8–10 pages | R67–R97 |
| **Challenge** | Timeboxed streak with daily prompts | 12–16 pages | R149–R247 |
| **Toolkit** | Mixed cards + worksheets + scripts | 12–16 pages | R179–R247 |

---

## Required pack pages (every type)

1. Cover  
2. Branding (who + promise)  
3. Contents  
4. Practice / content pages (type-shaped)  
5. Action sheets (max 7 actions)  
6. QR codes  
7. Upsell (soft)  
8. Thank-you

## Naming patterns

1. `[Outcome] Workbook`  
2. `The [Framework] Practice Pack` / Toolkit  
3. `[Pillar] 7-Day / 14-Day / 30-Day` planner or challenge  
4. `[Skill] Checklist` / `Cheat Sheet` / `Templates` / `Journal` / `Guide`

## Free vs paid

| Layer | Role |
|---|---|
| Episode free worksheet | One-pager lead magnet |
| Episode digital product | Paid (or soft-launch) expansion of the same framework |

Never invent new IP for the product. Expand the episode framework only.
