# SOP 06 — Stock Footage Selection

**Owner:** Founder  
**Cadence:** As needed  
**Estimated time:** 10 minutes (hard stop)

---

## Objective

Select license-safe, non-generic-feeling support footage that respects South African relevance and premium brand tone — preferably from CapCut’s library or your own reusable B-roll bank.

---

## Priority order (always)

1. **Own B-roll bank** (`04_Assets/broll/`)  
2. **CapCut built-in stock** (commercial license for YouTube)  
3. **Canva** media (check license for YouTube)  
4. Paid stock sites — **deferred** unless ROI clear  

---

## Step-by-step checklist

- [ ] List max **5** moments that need non-face visuals  
- [ ] Search own B-roll first (desk, hands, phone, notebook, SA light)  
- [ ] If gap: CapCut stock search with calm keywords (`city evening`, `typing`, `notebook`, `woman working laptop` — avoid “empowerment pose” clichés)  
- [ ] Reject: NYC skyline defaults, fake luxury cars, shocked stock faces, poverty porn, tribal caricature  
- [ ] Prefer contemporary African/urban/home-office texture when available  
- [ ] Favourite clips into CapCut for reuse  
- [ ] Note clip names in `04_Assets/music-sfx-notes.md` or `broll-index.md`  
- [ ] Stop at 10 minutes — teach with A-roll if stock fails  

---

## AI tools used

| Tool | Use |
|---|---|
| CapCut library search | Primary stock |
| ChatGPT | Optional keyword list for searches |

---

## Human review points

1. Does this look like *her* world?  
2. Would removing it hurt understanding? If no → skip.  

---

## Quality-control checklist

- [ ] License-safe for YouTube monetization  
- [ ] No copyrighted logos in focus unless fair teaching need  
- [ ] ≤ 5 stock inserts  
- [ ] Tone calm / premium  
- [ ] Timebox respected
