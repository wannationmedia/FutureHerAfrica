# SOP 11 — Community Posts

**Owner:** Founder  
**Cadence:** Batch-write weekly (15 min); paste on publish days (2 min)  
**Estimated time:** 15 minutes batch

---

## Objective

Keep The Forward Collective warm and curriculum-aligned using pre-written posts — never blank-page community management.

---

## Inputs

- Brief fields + community question  
- `templates/COMMUNITY_PACK.md`  
- Recycling pack community section

## Output

Week’s Community tab posts + WhatsApp announcement + Voice Classroom note script + newsletter stub

---

## Step-by-step checklist

- [ ] Duplicate `COMMUNITY_PACK.md` into episode folder  
- [ ] Monday — week intention (1–2 sentences + soft invite)  
- [ ] Midweek — amplify one member comment/question  
- [ ] Challenge day — monthly challenge reminder (no shame)  
- [ ] Friday — Forward Wins (member or micro-win)  
- [ ] Weekend — next episode tease  
- [ ] WhatsApp announcement: problem sentence + YouTube link  
- [ ] Voice Classroom: 60–90s tip script (record later on phone)  
- [ ] Newsletter: paste Idea / Skill / Action  
- [ ] Schedule paste times in calendar or note on run sheet  

---

## Tone rules

- Premium town square — not spam wall  
- One job per post  
- Invite thinking; don’t beg for likes  

---

## AI tools used

| Tool | Use |
|---|---|
| ChatGPT | Turn community question into 5 soft post variants |
| Google Docs | Batch store week pack |

---

## Human review points

1. Would you post this if you weren’t “growing”?  
2. Is any post just engagement bait with no teaching? Delete it.  

---

## Quality-control checklist

- [ ] All posts drafted on batch day  
- [ ] No link-spam walls  
- [ ] Permission considered for personal stories  
- [ ] WhatsApp + newsletter stubs ready  
- [ ] Tracker community = Y  

**Pass → weekly Definition of Done**
