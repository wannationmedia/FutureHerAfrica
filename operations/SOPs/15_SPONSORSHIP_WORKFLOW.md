# SOP 15 — Sponsorship Workflow

**Owner:** Founder  
**Cadence:** As earned (mid–late stage preference)  
**Estimated time:** Variable — protect weekly 4h production from sponsor chaos

---

## Objective

Run premium, few, deep brand partnerships that fund the mission without renting audience trust. Say no faster than yes.

---

## Inputs

- Inbound brief or outbound one-pager  
- Partner folder `07_Partners/sponsors`  
- Media kit (create once when ready)

## Output

Go/No-go decision → signed scope → integrated deliverable → invoice → post-report

---

## Step-by-step checklist

### Stage A — Qualify (15 min)

- [ ] Brand fit with FutureHer values?  
- [ ] Audience dignity protected (esp. money/body/fear)?  
- [ ] Product something you’d teach anyway?  
- [ ] Timeline compatible with ≤ 4h/week OS?  
- [ ] If any no → polite decline template  

### Stage B — Scope (30–45 min)

- [ ] Deliverables listed (1 integration video / Shorts / community / newsletter — pick few)  
- [ ] Exclusivity + category conflicts checked  
- [ ] Disclosure requirements agreed (FTC/ASA-equivalent honesty)  
- [ ] Fee + payment terms (ZAR) + kill fee  
- [ ] Revision limit (e.g. 1 round)  
- [ ] Save brief in `07_Partners/sponsors/YYYY_Brand/`  

### Stage C — Produce (inside or adjacent to weekly OS)

- [ ] Integrate into Template A–E without breaking brand system  
- [ ] Teach value before logo energy  
- [ ] Spoken + written disclosure  
- [ ] Sponsor gets draft once  
- [ ] Export + schedule per SOP 10  

### Stage D — Close (20 min)

- [ ] Deliver links + screenshots  
- [ ] Invoice  
- [ ] 7-day performance snapshot (views, CTR, comments themes) — not vanity theatre  
- [ ] Log learnings: renew / renegotiate / never again  

---

## Decline template (reuse)

> Thank you for thinking of FutureHer. We’re selective about partnerships so we can protect the trust of the women we teach. This one isn’t the right fit for our curriculum right now — wishing you a strong campaign.

## Quality-control checklist

- [ ] Disclosure clear  
- [ ] Brand system unbroken  
- [ ] No fake enthusiasm  
- [ ] Legal/claims from brand verified or refused  
- [ ] Weekly OS not permanently hijacked  

**Pass → deliver + invoice**
