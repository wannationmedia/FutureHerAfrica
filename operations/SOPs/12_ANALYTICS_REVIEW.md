# SOP 12 — Analytics Review

**Owner:** Founder  
**Cadence:** Monthly (not weekly)  
**Estimated time:** 30 minutes

---

## Objective

Extract **one** operational decision from data — what to repeat, fix, or stop — without turning analytics into a second job.

---

## Inputs

- YouTube Studio analytics (28 days)  
- Episode Tracker  
- Sheet: `08_Analytics/monthly-log`

## Output

3 bullets: Keep / Fix / Stop + optional template tweak note

---

## Step-by-step checklist

- [ ] Open YouTube Analytics → Last 28 days  
- [ ] Note: impressions CTR, average view duration %, traffic sources top 2  
- [ ] Identify top 3 and bottom 3 videos by retained audience  
- [ ] For top 3: what problem language + template (A–E) + hook pattern?  
- [ ] For bottom 3: was topic wrong, hook weak, or packaging off?  
- [ ] Check Shorts → long-form funnel (any Short driving subs/views?)  
- [ ] Skim comments for Problem Bank additions (≤ 10 new questions)  
- [ ] Decide **one** change for next month only  
- [ ] Log in Analytics sheet: date, keep, fix, stop  
- [ ] Refresh CTA links if broken  
- [ ] Stop at 30 minutes  

---

## Decision rules

| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| High CTR, low retention | Fix cold open / pacing — not thumbnails alone |
| Low CTR, good retention | Fix title/thumbnail packaging |
| Shorts views, no subs | Strengthen end CTA + series continuity |
| Comments ask same Q | Make that next episode |

---

## AI tools used

| Tool | Use |
|---|---|
| ChatGPT | Paste notes → “summarise into Keep/Fix/Stop for a calm educational brand” |
| YouTube Studio | Source of truth |

---

## Human review points

1. Did you invent a new process — or one concrete tweak?  
2. Are you chasing vanity spikes over trust topics?  

---

## Quality-control checklist

- [ ] Exactly one change committed  
- [ ] Problem Bank updated  
- [ ] No tool purchases decided from a single bad week  
- [ ] Logged  

**Pass → resume weekly production with one tweak**
