# SOP 03 — Fact Checking

**Owner:** Founder  
**Cadence:** Every episode (inside research/script block)  
**Estimated time:** 5–10 minutes

---

## Objective

Prevent trust damage. Verify claims before they become voice-over. Prefer understatement over impressive falsehoods.

---

## Inputs

- Script draft  
- Research source links  
- Any stats, product claims, legal/money/AI capability statements

## Output

Script marked `FACT-OK` or corrected lines with sources in brief

---

## Step-by-step checklist

- [ ] Highlight every number, “studies show”, “most women”, tool capability, money/return claim  
- [ ] For each highlight: verify on primary source **or** soften to lived observation  
- [ ] Money/fintech: no guaranteed returns; no illegal product advice  
- [ ] AI: state limits; no “AI will replace you overnight” fear  
- [ ] Career: no fake salary guarantees  
- [ ] Quotes: accurate attribution or remove  
- [ ] SA context: currency (ZAR), platforms, laws — don’t import US defaults blindly  
- [ ] If unverifiable in 2 minutes → rewrite as opinion/experience  
- [ ] Tick `FACT-OK` at top of script  

---

## Softening patterns (use freely)

| Risky | Safer |
|---|---|
| “Studies show 90%…” | “Many women tell me…” / “In practice I see…” |
| “This will double your income” | “This can clarify your next money decision” |
| “ChatGPT always…” | “In many cases ChatGPT can…” |

---

## AI tools used

| Tool | Use |
|---|---|
| ChatGPT | “List every factual claim in this script that needs a source” |
| Web search (via ChatGPT/browser) | Spot-check official docs only |

---

## Human review points

1. Would you defend this claim on a podcast with a sceptic?  
2. Does softening increase trust more than “impressive” numbers?  

---

## Quality-control checklist

- [ ] All hard claims sourced or softened  
- [ ] No medical/clinical prescriptions  
- [ ] No get-rich implications  
- [ ] Disclosures noted if affiliate tool mentioned  
- [ ] Script status = `FACT-OK`  

**Pass → SOP 04 Voice-over Production**
