# SOP 01 — Research

**Owner:** Founder  
**Cadence:** Every episode (weekly batch)  
**Estimated time:** 20 minutes (hard stop)

---

## Objective

Collect just enough truth, South African context, and audience language to teach one idea, one skill, and one action — without becoming a research project.

---

## Inputs

- Problem Bank (top unanswered question)
- Last 3 video comments / Ask Forward questions
- YouTube autocomplete (phrasing only)
- Official docs for any tool demoed

## Output

≤ 5 research bullets inside `templates/EPISODE_BRIEF.md`

---

## Step-by-step checklist

- [ ] Open Problem Bank → pick #1 unanswered question for this week  
- [ ] Write the **problem sentence** in her exact words  
- [ ] Assign pillar + CapCut template (A–E)  
- [ ] ChatGPT prompt: *“Given this SA women’s problem, list 5 teachable angles. Reject hustle/fear angles. Prefer calm systems.”*  
- [ ] Choose **one** angle only  
- [ ] Gather ≤ 5 supporting points (example, myth to kill, SA context, tool note, constraint)  
- [ ] Note 1 phone-first action she can finish in < 30 minutes  
- [ ] Note psychology beat in **one sentence** (support only — not clinical)  
- [ ] List source links for any statistic or “official” claim (max 3)  
- [ ] Stop at 20 minutes  

---

## AI tools used

| Tool | Use |
|---|---|
| ChatGPT | Angle shortlist, myth detection, SA framing suggestions |
| YouTube search | Autocomplete / “people also ask” phrasing |
| Cursor (optional) | Paste into brief template cleanly |

---

## Human review points

1. Is this *her* problem language — or creator jargon?  
2. Is the action actually doable on a phone this week?  
3. Would you still teach this if zero “trending” data existed?  

---

## Quality-control checklist

- [ ] ≤ 5 bullets total  
- [ ] No clinical diagnosis framing  
- [ ] SA relevance present (cost, data, language, workplace, culture)  
- [ ] Sources noted for any hard claim  
- [ ] Ready for Script SOP without more research  

**Pass → SOP 02 Script Writing + SOP 03 Fact Checking**
