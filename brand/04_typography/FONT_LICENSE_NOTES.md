| **Source Serif 4** | Google Fonts | SIL OFL | Workbook / article subheads |
| **Plus Jakarta Sans** | Canva / Google Fonts | Check Canva + OFL terms | Emergency Canva only |

## Rules

1. Prefer Satoshi everywhere production allows.  
2. Embed fonts in ebook/PDF exports.  
3. Keep licence PDFs/screenshots in Drive `00_Brand/04_typography/licences/`.  
4. Do not use unpaid retail fonts in public brand assets.
