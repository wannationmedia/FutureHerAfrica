## Hard law

1. Do **not** redesign her.  
2. Do **not** replace her.  
3. Do **not** create a new character.  
4. Do **not** invent a mascot.  
5. Do **not** change her identity.  

The approved EP001 host visual is the **canonical reference**. Every future host appearance must be recognisably the same woman.

## 2. Visual character

She must read as:

| Trait | On-screen meaning |
|---|---|
| **Natural African woman** | Contemporary, dignified, everyday excellence — not costume tourism |
| **Intelligent** | Clear eyes, calm focus, teaching presence |
| **Approachable** | Warm closed-lip or soft open smile; never cold or superior |
| **Composed & confident** | Quiet authority; no panic, no rage-point, no fake cry |
| **Professional but not corporate** | Ready for work and life; not boardroom armour or luxury flex |
| **Warm & relatable** | Someone the audience would trust for a 30-minute practice |
| **Human-first** | Technology supports her lesson; she is never a tech prop |

Tone match: FutureHer academy calm — “Ready for what’s next.”

## 3. Consistency rules

Across **episodes, thumbnails, Shorts, presentations, ebooks, and social graphics**:

| Keep locked | Rule |
|---|---|
| Recognisable face | Same woman as EP001 thumbnail — squint test must pass |
| Facial proportions | Eyes, nose, mouth spacing and face shape stay stable |
| Skin tone | Same rich warm medium-dark brown family |
| Age presentation | Stay late-20s to mid-30s; do not age her into a new identity |
| Realism level | Premium photographic / editorial realism — not cartoon, not hyper-CGI doll |
| Hair identity | Natural black textured hair; styles may vary slightly but must not read as a different person |
| Expression band | Calm confidence · knowing warmth · soft smile · thoughtful focus — never shock-mouth |

**Forbidden regeneration habit:** Do not open a new prompt and accept “a different South African woman who feels similar.” Similarity is not continuity. Continuity means **this** host.

**Platform rule:** Whenever the generator supports reference images / character reference / face lock, attach one of the primary EP001 host assets above.

## 4. Allowed variations

The same host may change:

| Allowed | Guidance |
|---|---|
| Clothing | Blouses, soft knits, simple dresses, smart-casual layers in Horizon-adjacent colours (Ink, Stone, Ivory, Lagoon, Brass accents). Avoid costume, logo spam, neon fashion |
| Pose | Head-and-shoulders, three-quarter, seated desk, standing teach, walking calm — still clearly her |
| Facial expression | Soft smile, thoughtful, listening, slight knowing look — stay inside §3 expression band |
| Camera angle | Eye-level preferred; slight high/low OK; avoid extreme wide distortion of the face |
| Environment | Home office, desk, calm SA interior, soft outdoor SA light, academy-adjacent spaces |
| Lighting | Natural soft daylight / warm-neutral editorial light appropriate to the scene; grade toward `FH_Grade_Horizon` |

**Test:** If you cover the logo and series pill, a returning viewer should still say “that’s the FutureHer host.”

## 6. Brand relationship

| Truth | Practice |
|---|---|
| She **represents** FutureHerAfrica | Recurring digital host / mentor face |
| She is **not** the brand itself | Wordmark, Horizon arc, FH monogram remain the brand system |
| She does not replace teaching visuals | Keep diagrams, screen recordings, hands-at-work, real African contexts |
| She does not own every frame | B-roll, worksheets, action cards, and frameworks carry the lesson |

Use her for: thumbnails, host inserts, Shorts hooks, presentation openers, ebook/social face moments when a human mentor presence is needed.  
Do not force her into every diagram or every product page.

## 7. Generation standard

1. Start from `brand/FH_HOST_GENERATION_PROMPT.txt`.  
2. Fill `[SCENE] [POSE] [EXPRESSION] [WARDROBE] [BACKGROUND] [CAMERA] [LIGHTING]`.  
3. Attach EP001 canonical reference image when the platform allows.  
4. Append the Negative Prompt block every time.  
5. Generate options → keep only frames that pass the **same-host squint test**.  
6. Grade toward `FH_Grade_Horizon` before CapCut / Canva import.  
7. Log seed + platform + which reference file was used in the episode asset note.

**Rejection rule:** If identity drifts, regenerate — do not “fix it in post” into a new face.
