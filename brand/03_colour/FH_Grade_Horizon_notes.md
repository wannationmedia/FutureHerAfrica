# FH_Grade_Horizon — Colour Grade Notes

**Use in:** CapCut adjustment layer / LUT ≤30% intensity  
**Authority:** Brand Design Studio §§7.3, 10.2

## Recipe

1. Exposure: natural, faces first  
2. Contrast: medium  
3. Temperature: +1 to +2 warm  
4. Tint: slight green-away from magenta influencer look  
5. Highlights: soft roll-off  
6. Shadows: slight lift (keep Ink blacks from crushing)  
7. Skin: protect — no orange plastic  

## Series micro-bias (optional, subtle)

| Series | Bias |
|---|---|
| Educational / AI | Cleaner; Lagoon-friendly midtones |
| Money | Cleaner highlights; Brass-friendly |
| Career | Most editorial / neutral |
| Relationships | Warmest; protect skin |
| Stories | Atmospheric; longer holds in edit |

## Do not

- Heavy influencer LUT packs  
- Neon cyber grades  
- Orange beauty-filter skin  
- Crushing blacks that kill SA night/indoor truth
